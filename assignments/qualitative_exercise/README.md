# Qualitative research exercise

**Purpose:** Design and transparently analyze a small approved qualitative or document-based inquiry.

## Learning outcomes

Align question, theory, evidence, and method; document decisions; interpret evidence cautiously;
meet ethical, privacy, AI-disclosure, and reproducibility expectations.

## Task and required files

Complete the version announced on Cyber Campus. Required files: Protocol, approved/deidentified evidence or access note, coding table, analysis, reflexivity and ethics statement.

## Submission

Submit through Cyber Campus unless the instructor explicitly names another channel. Confirm
filenames and open submitted files after upload.

## Grading criteria — draft for instructor approval

Question/design alignment; literature/evidence quality; transparent method; interpretation;
limitations; ethics/privacy; documentation and reproducibility; communication. Exact point
allocations are not final until instructor approval.

## AI, reproducibility, and privacy

AI is allowed only when the released assignment says so; disclose permitted use and verification.
Work must run from its stated starting point. Do not submit or publish identifiable, confidential,
copyrighted, licensed, restricted, or raw sensitive material.

## Common mistakes

Treating a topic as a question; listing sources instead of synthesizing; choosing a method before
the question; overstating a result; hiding exclusions or transformations; unverified AI output;
local-only paths; and missing access/license notes.

## Student checklist

- [ ] I followed the released Cyber Campus version.
- [ ] Every central claim has evidence.
- [ ] Method and evidence fit the question.
- [ ] Sources and AI assistance are verified and disclosed.
- [ ] Privacy and data-use restrictions are respected.
- [ ] Files reproduce from the stated starting point.
