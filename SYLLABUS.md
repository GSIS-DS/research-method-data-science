# Syllabus guide

The [final syllabus PDF](TIC3002_syllabus_2026Fall.pdf) is authoritative. This page is a readable navigation
aid and does not replace it.

- Course: Research Method of Data Science (TIC3002), 3 credits
- Semester: Fall 2026
- Instructor: Iegor Vyshnevskyi
- Time: Tuesday/Thursday, 13:30–14:45
- Audience: primarily third-year GSIS undergraduates
- Prerequisites: one university statistics course; basic algebra, functions, graphs, and introductory
  economic reasoning. Programming experience is helpful but not required.

## Purpose

Students conduct a complete study: question, theory, literature, method choice, qualitative and/or
quantitative evidence, analysis, verification, paper, presentation, and reproducible supporting
materials. See the [course schedule](COURSE_SCHEDULE.md), [assessment pages](assignments/README.md),
and [AI policy](docs/ai_use_and_verification.md).

## Important policy summary

Permitted AI use must be disclosed and verified. AI is normally prohibited during the midterm.
Protect sensitive data. Work must run from its documented starting point. Cyber Campus is official
for announcements, deadlines, grades, and submissions.

Research workflow labs and participation carry 15%, bringing the detailed assessment components to 100%.
