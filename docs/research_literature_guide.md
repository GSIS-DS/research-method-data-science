# Literature search and synthesis

Record database/source, exact search string, date, filters, inclusion/exclusion decisions, full
citation, question, data/context, method, finding, limitation, relevance, theme/debate, and whether
the original source was verified. A review compares and evaluates bodies of evidence to build an
argument; it is not a sequence of paper summaries. See the [worked contrast](../examples/example_literature_matrix/README.md).
