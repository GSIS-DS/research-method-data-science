# Final individual research project guide

The project is the course's organizing feature. It is not necessarily quantitative or mixed-methods.
The question determines appropriate evidence and method, subject to course requirements and
instructor approval.

## Required paper components

1. Introduction
2. Research problem and question
3. Motivation and contribution
4. Literature review
5. Literature gap
6. Conceptual or theoretical framework
7. Data or qualitative evidence
8. Methods
9. Results
10. Discussion
11. Conclusion
12. Limitations
13. References
14. AI-use disclosure
15. Reproducible supporting materials

Each claim must be traceable to evidence; each transformation and analytical decision must be
documented. See the [project repository template](../templates/final_project_repository/README.md)
and [reproducibility guide](reproducibility_guide.md).

## Privacy boundary

Do not upload confidential interviews, identifiable personal information, copyrighted material,
restricted datasets, or raw sensitive material to a public repository. When data cannot be shared,
provide a lawful access description, a data dictionary, transformation outline, and reconstruction
instructions without disclosing protected material.
