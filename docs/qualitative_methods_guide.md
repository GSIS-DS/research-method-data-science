# Qualitative methods

Qualitative research can investigate meaning, process, experience, context, and mechanisms through
case study, interview, focus group, observation, and document analysis. Design requires justified
sampling and access, consent and privacy, researcher reflexivity, transparent coding, development of
categories/themes, trustworthiness checks, triangulation where appropriate, and explicit limitations.
Mixed-methods integration requires explaining how evidence types relate, not merely placing them
side by side. Do not collect identifiable human-subject data without instructor approval and ethical
safeguards. Use the [fictional coding example](../examples/example_qualitative_coding/README.md).
