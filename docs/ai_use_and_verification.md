# Responsible AI use and verification

AI may be used only when an assignment permits it. Every permitted use must be disclosed. You
remain responsible for every claim, citation, line of code, and result. Locate and read the original
source behind every AI-suggested citation. Fabricated sources, data, results, or hidden AI-generated
work are misconduct. You must be able to explain and reproduce submitted work. AI is normally
prohibited during the midterm unless explicitly authorized.

## Verification checklist

- **Code:** read each line; test a small known case; restart and run all; inspect warnings.
- **Factual claims:** confirm with authoritative/original sources and record them.
- **Citations:** locate, read, and verify authors, title, year, and relevance.
- **Statistics:** check assumptions, units, uncertainty, effect direction, and substantive meaning.
- **Sensitivity:** vary a reasonable choice and report whether the conclusion changes.
- **Transformations:** compare row counts, keys, missingness, ranges, and duplicates before/after.

Use the [AI-use log](../templates/ai_use_log.md) even when the final disclosure is shorter.
