# Data ethics and privacy

Use the minimum data needed. Confirm lawful access, license, consent, and purpose. Do not collect
identifiable human-subject data without instructor approval and appropriate ethical safeguards.
Never place confidential interviews, contact details, credentials, restricted data, or raw sensitive
material in a public repository. Deidentify cautiously: removing names alone may not prevent
reidentification. Record exclusions, transformations, and access limits. When sharing is prohibited,
share metadata, synthetic examples, and reconstruction guidance instead.
