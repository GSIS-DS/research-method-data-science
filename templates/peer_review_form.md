# Structured peer review

Reviewer: [name]  
Project: [title]

1. State the research question in your own words.
2. Identify the strongest question–evidence–method alignment.
3. Identify one unsupported leap or ambiguity and point to its location.
4. Check whether limitations match the design.
5. Test one reproducibility instruction.
6. Suggest one specific revision; the author decides whether and how to use it.
