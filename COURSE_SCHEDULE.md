# Course schedule

Classes meet every Tuesday and Thursday from 13:30 to 14:45. 

| Week | Tuesday | Thursday | Main project connection |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research types and course orientation | Colab, GitHub, notebooks, organization, reproducibility | Setup |
| 2 | Problems, questions, theory, concepts, contribution | Individual question clinic | Preliminary question |
| 3 | Finding and evaluating literature | Documented literature-search lab | Search log and evidence matrix |
| 4 | Literature synthesis, gaps, frameworks | Synthesis and proposal workshop | Literature review and proposal |
| 5 | Introduction to qualitative research | Design, sampling, protocols, ethics | Qualitative design |
| 6 | Coding and interpreting qualitative evidence | Qualitative coding lab | Qualitative exercise |
| 7 | Quantitative research design and validity | Individual design clinic and review | Data-method alignment |
| 8 | Midterm practical assessment | Official assessment arrangement | Individual competence |
| 9 | Finding and documenting quantitative data | Data-source lab | Dataset and source log |
| 10 | Data preparation and descriptive research | Cleaning and visualization studio | Reproducible pipeline |
| 11 | Bivariate analysis and inference | Simulation and inference lab | Appropriate evidence |
| 12 | Regression as a research method | Regression and diagnostics lab | Main analysis |
| 13 | Causal reasoning, prediction, and text as data | Method choice and AI verification | Robustness and verification |
| 14 | Writing the complete research paper | Full-draft and reproducibility workshop | Complete draft |
| 15 | Presentations and Q&A, Group 1 | Presentations and Q&A, Group 2 | Complete-study presentation |
| 16 | Presentations and Q&A, Group 3 | Revision workshop and final submission | Final project |
